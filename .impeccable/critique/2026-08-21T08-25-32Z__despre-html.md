## Design specificity verdict

Two pages stitched together, and the seam is where the client pointed. Above the
portrait nothing could be lifted to another business: the drawn spine, the three
ages of the same tree, watercolour photo edges, no radius, no shadow. Below the
portrait the world stops: the carousel and (formerly) the interview cards are
rounded, shadowed default cards with chrome arrows and a dot row, breaking the
rule DESIGN.md records for the landing page's photos ("no border-radius, no
overflow, no box-shadow - photos sit flat on cream").

That is why the client dislikes the carousel and cannot say why: it is the first
thing on the page that looks like it came from somewhere else.

Deterministic scan: NO SIGNAL. detect.mjs ran degraded (htmlparser2, css-select,
css-tree, domutils missing). Empty finding list is an undercount, not a pass.
Visual overlays: none attempted.

## What is working

- The drawn spine is design thinking, not decoration. despre.js measures real
  boxes, computes free paper, caps sway at 60px with a 14px guard, alternates
  per block, one continuous curve. Degrades correctly without script.
- The portrait band's restraint: bleeding square photo, sand rule, hung opening
  quotation mark, cream ground with hairlines because the two creams are 1.03
  apart.
- No sideways scroll at 1280 or 390. All 25 flagged bleeds are deliberate clip.

## Priority issues

### [P0] The page never asks for anything
main contains ZERO links (verified). PRODUCT.md principle 4 makes contact the
only visitor action; this is the most persuasive page and the only one that
never asks. Fix: closing band after .talks built from the page's own materials,
one --action button to contact.html.
TRAP: despre.js reveal watcher uses rootMargin '0px 0px -12% 0px' and has NO
atBottom guard (main.js does). DESIGN.md documents this bug. A new final band
will be invisible without a second inset-free observer.
Command: /impeccable shape

### [P1] Two videos cannot be stopped, one cannot be started
Both loop, controls suppressed, no reduced-motion branch in any of despre.js's
five jobs. base.css's blanket rule does nothing to a <video>. WCAG 2.2.2.
Also: main.js:35 targets .hero__film but this page uses .story__film, so the
iOS autoplay-recovery never runs here; and the HTML comment promises a
bottom-right control that does not exist on this page.
Command: /impeccable harden

### [P1] On a phone the carousel is smaller than a thumbnail
--slide: clamp(16rem, 68vw, 46rem) gives 265x149px at 390px viewport, 62px of
empty cream each side, two 48px control discs overlapping the photo corners by
7-8px. At 1280 the arrows float ~200px out in empty paper (pinned to the page
gutter, not the slide). Bug: sizes claims 88vw while layout uses 68vw.
Fix: raise slide to ~88vw on phone (also makes sizes honest); move arrows onto
the slide's edges or drop them on phone; drop radius+shadow so photos sit flat;
hide rather than disable prev.
Command: /impeccable layout

### [P1] Carousel dots announce a keyboard contract they do not honour
despre.js:374 sets role="tablist" with aria-selected on each dot. No
role="tabpanel", no aria-controls, no roving tabindex (all verified). Arrow keys
do nothing. Dots carry both aria-current and aria-selected. Honest markup is a
plain group of buttons; the ul.reel__track with tabindex=0 is already correct.
Command: /impeccable harden

### [P2] Section-title device renders three ways and vanishes on a phone
roots (title between two hairlines, borrowed by reel), talks (centred, no
rules), keeper (left-aligned). At 390 the two .roots__rule hairlines measure
0px wide (flex: 1 1 0, two-line title eats the row); the reel's survive at 50px
because its title is one line.
Fix: min-width ~2.5rem on the rule, cap the title width so it wraps.
Command: /impeccable typeset

## Persona red flags

- Wholesale buyer on a phone: convinced by the portrait, decides to call,
  nothing to press. Then reads NUME DE COMPLETAT under the owner's face.
- Reduced-motion visitor: full-bleed drone loop, no pause, no stop, no hide.
- Screen-reader user at the carousel: promised a tablist, given six buttons
  that ignore arrow keys.
- First-time visitor: at 390 the header is a leaf mark and a burger; the h1 is
  a mood, not an identity; the farm's NAME does not appear until the footer,
  ~4,900px down.

## Questions to consider

1. Does the carousel earn its place at all? Six photographs behind a control
   surface, for a visitor PRODUCT.md says spends a short time. What if they were
   simply laid on the paper, loose and uneven, with painted edges, no arrows,
   no dots? "Viata din livada" is not one photograph at a time.
2. If you deleted everything below the closing quote and put the contact
   invitation there, would the page be worse? It would end on its peak. Perhaps
   the interviews move up, above the portrait, as evidence.
3. The three tree drawings grow sapling to mature. Does any visitor notice? It
   is the best idea on the page and it is never named.
4. Why is a page about a farm opened by a picture taken from 200 metres above
   it? PRODUCT.md says the claim is taste, not scale.
